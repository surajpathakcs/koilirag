Fonepay is Nepal’s largest payment network licensed and regulated by Nepal Rastra Bank as a Payment System Operator (PSO).

Interoperable payment network of Nepal and beyond

Fonepay connects Banking and Financial Institutions, Digital Wallets, Consumers, and Merchants in its interoperable network and facilitates various Person-to-Person (P2P) and Person-to-Merchant (P2M) payments and offers other services aligning with instant, easy and convenient money movement.

This interoperable network connects over 60+ partners with over 20mn + registered customers and 1.7mn + merchants in Nepal. Fonepay QR is EMV compliant, which is the most widely accepted standard for QR payments.
