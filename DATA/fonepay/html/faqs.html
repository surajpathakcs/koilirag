Frequently Asked Questions

Explore our comprehensive FAQs to enhance your Fonepay experience and ensure smooth, secure transactions at your fingertips.

What is Fonepay QR?

Fonepay QR is a digital payment solution that utilizes Quick Response (QR) codes, allowing users to make secure and convenient transactions by scanning QR codes with their smartphones. Fonepay QR is EMV compliant, which is the most widely accepted standard for QR payments.

How can you use Fonepay QR?

Step 1: Login to your mobile banking app.

Step 2: Swipe right to open QR Scan or tap on the ‘QR Scan’ option.

Step 3: Scan the QR Code.

Step 4: Enter the amount to pay and other necessary details.

Step 5: Check the details and tap on “Confirm”.

Step 6: Enter transaction PIN or use your biometric to execute the payment.

Is it safe to use Fonepay QR?

Fonepay QR is secure to use as it employs advanced encryption and authentication protocols, ensuring the safety of your transactions.

How merchant can install Fonepay QR in their businesses?

Merchant can install Fonepay QR in their businesses by reaching out to their respective bank.

Banking apps that support Fonepay QR?

Fonepay QR is accessible through 64 BFIs (Banking & Financial Institutions) including digital wallets of Nepal.

Are there any fees involved in using Fonepay QR?

There are no fees involved in using Fonepay QR, both for customers and merchants.

How do I get notified about my transaction?

You get notified about your QR transaction through SMS and mobile banking app notification.

How can merchants check payments received and settled? 

Merchants can check payments received through either SMS or Fonepay Business App.

What is Fonepay Direct?

Fonepay Direct is a service offered by Fonepay that enables a mobile banking user to conveniently transfer funds from their own bank account to a chosen recipient's bank account, regardless of whether the two accounts are held at the same bank or different banks.

How can I send money using Fonepay Direct?

Step 1: Login to your mobile banking app.

Step 2: Navigate to the "send money" section & tap on “Fund transfer”.

Step 3: Choose the option of sending money either from the same bank or a different bank.

Step 4: Enter the necessary details.

Step 5: Check the details and tap on “confirm”.

What are the different ways of sending money using Fonepay Direct?

There are three ways of sending money using Fonepay Direct.

  1. You can send the money by entering the recipient’s mobile number.
  2. You can send the money by entering the recipient’s bank account number.
  3. You also have the choice to send the money by scanning the recipient's personal QR code associated with their account.

Is Fonepay Direct safe to use?

Fonepay Direct ensures a high level of security for your transactions. The service utilizes encryption and advanced security measures to protect your financial information and prevent unauthorized access.

Are there any fees involved in using Fonepay Direct?

It does involve fees. For each transaction, a charge of Rs 10 is applicable, but only when the sender's bank differs from the recipient's bank. Notably, if both the sender's and recipient's bank accounts belong to the same bank, there is no fee associated with this service.

Does Fonepay Direct offer a Fonepay Points like Fonepay QR?

Fonepay Direct does not offer a Fonepay Points like Fonepay QR.

Banks that support Fonepay Direct?

Fonepay Direct is accessible through 64 BFIs (Banking & Financial Institutions) including digital wallets of Nepal.

What if I encounter a problem with Fonepay Direct?

In case you come across any problems with Fonepay Direct, reach out to your respective bank.

What is Fonepay Bills?

Fonepay Bills is one of the services of Fonepay through which customers can directly pay their bills for Electricity, Water, TV, Internet, EMI, Government Revenue, Education, Airlines, and more with just a few taps.

How can I purchase airline tickets using Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Domestic Flights” or “International Flight”.
  4. Choose “Airlines” & “Flight time”.
  5. Fill the necessary details.
  6. Check the details and proceed for “Payment”.

How can I pay electricity bills through Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Electricity” service.
  4. Fill your information of “NEA Counter”, “SC Number/ID” & proceed to “Inquiry”.
  5. Check the details and proceed for “Payment”.

How can I pay Internet bills through Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Internet” service.
  4. Select your ISP service.
  5. Enter your username and select your package.
  6. Check the details and confirm “Payment”.

What is Fonepay App?

Fonepay App is an upgrade to the previous Fonepay Offer App with the most awaited Payment Feature. With this app, users can link their bank accounts and wallets and transfer funds to contacts and make payments at various merchants by scanning QR codes or by Fonepay Tap and Go (FoneTAG). It also features other features like earning rewards as you spend, get informed about exclusive offers and redeem at the available merchants.

How can I log in to Fonepay App?

You can log in to Fonepay App by simply creating an account or signing up with previously made account. Once you log in, you can see your profile with the “Fonepay Points” you have received so far in the app.

What is Bank Link or Account Link?

Bank Link or Account Link is a feature that connects your bank accounts and wallets to Fonepay App. You can link multiple accounts and wallets in your Fonepay App. You can access any linked accounts and make payments and fund transfers from any desired accounts.

How can I link my Bank Account or Wallet?

  1. Download and sign in on the Fonepay App.
  2. On the home screen, tap “Link Your Accounts”. You will be provided with the list of Banks or Wallets to choose from.
  3. Tap on your desired bank/ wallets and input your account details.
  4. You will be directed towards the Mobile Banking App of the bank or Wallet Apps.
  5. Log in with your account details and enter OTP.
  6. The desired account shall be linked.

My Account Link is unsuccessful. What can be the reason?

There can be various reasons for unsuccessful Account Links. While linking your account or wallet, please make sure:

  1. Your Registered Mobile Number at Wallet or Bank is SAME as registered in Fonepay App.
  2. Your registered Full Name at Wallet or Bank is SAME as registered in Fonepay App.

Can I link multiple accounts? What should I ensure for successful account links?

You can link multiple bank accounts and wallets in your Fonepay App. However, you need to make sure that the registered number in all the accounts is SAME as the number registered in your Fonepay App.

I have multiple Bank Accounts, but their mobile banking numbers are different. Can I link them all?

Having the same registered mobile number is a MUST for account link. The mobile number registered in Fonepay App, and mobile banking app of all your banks must be the same. You may contact your bank branch to change your registered number for the mobile banking app.

Are there any charges associated with the App?

There are no charges for creating Fonepay App Account and linking your bank accounts or wallets. But charges for fund transfers and transactions are levied as per prevailing Fonepay Rules and Regulations.

Will Fonepay App have access to my bank account details if I link accounts?

Fonepay App can only act as a platform. Hence your linked banks accounts and wallets are completely secure.

Transaction limit when I make payments from Fonepay App?

You can make payments of up to NPR. 2,00,000 (QR and IBFT Combined) per linked bank account on a daily basis.

Can I see statements? Will it show all the statements even after I unlink bank?

You can see the payment statements in the Fonepay App. Look for “Statements” and tap to get complete details of your payments initiated from the app.

If you unlink your bank account, then your statements will be lost. However, once you re-link the same account, you can access your statements.

Do I get Fonepay points paying from fonepay app whether it’s online or offline?

You will earn your Fonepay Points by making payments from Fonepay App, be it online or offline.

Can I make HUB Payments from Fonepay App?

Currently there are no features of HUB Payments in Fonepay App. Stay tuned for upgrades.

What is FoneTAG?

FoneTAG is an offline payment solution that enables customers to make merchant payments by presenting NFC tag or a CPQR code (customer presented QR code) from mobile banking app even in areas with limited or no internet connectivity.

What is “Offline Token”?

Offline Token is a one-time authorization token generated to enable offline payments. With issuance of this token, you can start making offline payments.

How can I enable offline payments?

To enable offline payments,

  1. Go to Home Page or “Accounts” Page and Tap in “Offline Payments”
  2. Select your preferred Linked Bank Account
  3. Tap on “Activate”
  4. Enter the OTP you have received in your phone
  5. Offline Payment is now enabled.

How can I make offline payments?

After enabling offline payment, Log out from your account.

  1. On your Login Page, tap “Offline Payment”
  2. Select either of the options – FoneTAG or Customer Presented QR to make offline payment
  3. If your mobile phone supports NFC Technology, then tap “FoneTAG” and place your phone at the Merchant Terminal. The merchant will receive the payment.
  4. If your mobile phone does not support NFC Technology, then tap “Generate QR” and ask your merchant to scan your QR Code. Your payment will be successful.

What is NFC tag? 

With NFC tag, you can make offline payments at various NFC enabled Merchant Terminals with just one TAP. This feature requires enabling NFC and Contactless Payments in your phone. Look out for NFC symbols at the terminals.

What is Customer Presented QR?

Customer Presented QR is an alternative to NFC and an option for offline payments, if your phone does not support NFC and Contactless Payments. You can show this QR to Merchants, who can scan and receive payments on your behalf.

Is FoneTAG or Customer Presented QR Safe?

Yes, since it uses time-based tokenization and encryption modules during payments, these offline payments are safe. For offline payments, you can make payments up to Rs. 5000 per transaction.

How do I know if a merchant accepts offline payment?

Since it is a new technology, only selected merchants accept Offline Payments. Look out for NFC Symbols or ask the cashier to know the availability of Offline Payments from Fonepay App.

What is Two Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a security process in which a user is required to provide two forms of identification in order to access a system or service. The two forms of identification are typically something the user knows (such as a password), and something the user has (such as a security token or a code sent to a mobile phone).

What is the purpose of Two Factor Authentication?

The purpose of 2FA is to provide an additional layer of security beyond just a password, making it more difficult for an unauthorized person to access the system or service.

How can I enable Two Factor Authentication in Business App?

Step 1: Find “Security” option at Account setting  

Step 2: Click “Two Factor Authentication” and select either “SMS” or “TOTP” 

Step 3: For “Two Factor Authentication”, enter your mobile number to receive the OTP.  

Step 4: Verify and proceed.

What are the differences between “OTP” and “TOTP”?

OTP: An OTP is a password that is valid for only one login session or transaction, on a computer system or other digital device. This password is generated by a secure server or a specialized hardware token, and is sent to the user via SMS or email.

TOTP: This password is generated by a software token, such as a mobile app, that is installed on the user's device. The software token uses the current time to calculate the TOTP and displays it on the user's device.

Can Merchants also be offline while accepting foneTAG payments?

No, to receive offline payments from your customers, you as a merchant need to be online either through Fonepay Certified POS Devices or Fonepay Business App. Only Customers can be offline during payments.

What are various ways of receiving Offline Payments?

There are two ways to receive offline payments – NFC Based FoneTAG and QR Based Customer Presented QR.

What is FoneTAG?

FoneTAG means Fonepay Tap & Go. This is an offline feature to receive the payment from your customer through your business app and POS device by using Near Field Communication Channel.

How do I receive the payment through FoneTAG in Business App?

You can receive payment from your customer through this app if your smartphone supports NFC (Near Field Communication) technology.  

Step 1: Log in to your app, select ‘receive payment’ option from home screen.

Step 2: It shows you the FoneTAG option (If your smartphone has NFC technology) 

Step 3: Ask your customer to select FoneTAG option from Offline Features available at Fonepay App.  

Step 4: Click on FoneTAG option from your business app.  

Step 5: Place your smartphone near the smartphone of customer. 

What is Customer Presented QR (CPQR)?

Customer Presented QR is an alternative to FoneTAG and an option for offline payments, if your customer’s phone or your device does not support NFC and Contactless Payments.

How do I receive the payment from Customer presented QR (CPQR)?

Step 1: Ask your customer to show their QR that they have activated from Fonepay app.

Step 2: Select ‘Receive Payment’ option from your business app after log in.

Step 3: Find ‘Scan Customer’s QR code’ option and select it.

Step 4: Enter the amount.

Step 5: Scan Customer’s QR to receive the amount.

How do I enable Offline Payments – FoneTAG or CPQR in my app and POS device?

To enable Offline Payments - FoneTAG or Customer Presented QR in your business, please contact and request your bank. These offline payments can be enabled in your Fonepay Certified POS Devices or Fonepay Business App.

Can I scan customer presented QR (CPQR) through POS device?

Yes, you can scan CPQR through your Fonepay Certified POS device. Make sure to enable this feature in your device by contacting your bank.

Is there any transaction limit to receive payment through CPQR and FoneTAG features?

Yes, there is a transaction limit worth Rs 5000 (per transaction) to receive payment through CPQR and FoneTAG feature.

What is a Fonepay Credit Card?

Fonepay Credit Card is a fully functional digital/virtual card solution which empowers card holders with unparalleled convenience, flexibility, and control over their credit card functionality. This card allows users to scan and pay at any Fonepay QR merchant across the country.

How can I apply for a Fonepay Credit Card?

Eligible or pre-approved users can apply directly through your bank’s mobile banking application. Simply log in the app, tap Fonepay Credit Card banner on the homepage, and follow the application steps.

Who is eligible for a Fonepay Credit Card?

Eligibility for the Fonepay Credit Card depends on the issuing bank’s policies and credit evaluation process. In general, some of the common criteria may include:

  • Being an existing customer of the issuing bank
  • Stable income source or payroll account with the bank
  • Satisfactory credit history and transaction record
  • Maintaining a fixed deposit or loan relationship with the bank

We recommend checking with your respective bank for the exact eligibility requirements.

Do I need to visit the bank to get this card?

No. The Fonepay Credit Card is 100% digital. You can apply, receive, and use the card directly from your mobile banking application.

How can I apply if Fonepay Credit Card banner is not visible in my Mobile Banking App?

Fonepay Credit Card banner is visible to eligible/pre-approved users who meet the bank’s eligibility criteria. If you don’t see the banner, please contact your bank to request the card.

Where can I use the Fonepay Credit Card?

  • Any Fonepay QR merchant nationwide
  • Online payments (e-commerce websites, apps, services) 

Why doesn’t my QR work when I scan with Fonepay Credit Card?

This usually happens if you are scanning a non-Fonepay QR or a personal QR code (Fonepay Direct Personal QR). Fonepay Credit Card works only with Fonepay-registered business QRs. Please make sure the merchant is displaying an official Fonepay Business QR.

How is it different from a regular credit card?

Fonepay Credit Card is a virtual (digital-only) card, offering smarter features compared to a physical card:

  • No physical plastic card – stored securely in your mobile banking app.
  • Instant issuance – get your card instantly without visiting the bank.
  • Scan & Pay – pay instantly by scanning any Fonepay Business QR.
  • Real-time transaction view – track every spend instantly from your app.
  • Check your credit limit anytime – see your available balance and usage in real time.

Can I get a physical card if needed?

Currently, the Fonepay Credit Card is a virtual/digital-only card.

What is the credit limit of the card?

Your credit limit is determined by your bank based on your financial profile. You can view your assigned limit directly in the mobile banking app.

What is the charge for merchants accepting Fonepay QR?

Merchants are not charged any fees for transactions below Rs. 2,000. For transactions above Rs. 2,000, a 0.6% fee applies, with a maximum cap of Rs. 300 per transaction.

What is the charge for users using Fonepay QR?

Making payments with the Fonepay Credit Card is completely free. However, annual charges or other fees may apply as per the issuing bank’s credit card policy.

Fonepay Credit Card is currently available through our partnered banks: Citizens Bank International, Everest Bank, Kumari Bank, Laxmi Sunrise Bank, Mahalaxmi Bikas Bank, and Shine Resunga Development Bank. Other banks are in the process of onboarding, so availability may expand soon.

Fonepay Credit Card is currently limited to domestic use at Fonepay QR-enabled merchants within Nepal.

How do I make bill payments for the Fonepay Credit Card?

Credit card bills can be paid directly through your mobile banking app by tapping Pay Card.

What happens if I fail to make a repayment on time?

Late payments may incur interest or penalties based on the bank's credit policy. It is advisable to review the terms during application or consult with the bank.

Is Fonepay Credit Card safe?

Yes. It’s fully digital, secured by your mobile banking app, and backed by Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard which is globally recognized benchmark for payment security.

Who is an Issuer Bank?

Issuer Bank is a bank in which cardholder has his/her account and holds Fonepay card issued by the Bank.

Who is an Acquirer Bank?

The Bank which has acquired the merchant or has enabled the merchant to accept payment through Fonepay Cards.
