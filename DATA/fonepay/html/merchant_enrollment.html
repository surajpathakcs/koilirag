Fonepay QR

Merchant Enrollment Form Request

Fonepay QR enables secure, cashless payments at over 1.7 million merchant locations across Nepal, making it the most widely accepted QR payment system in Nepal.

By becoming a Fonepay merchant, you can accept payments directly from customers through all major mobile banking apps and e-wallets without the need for a costly POS machine—just a simple QR code is enough. It’s fast, safe, and convenient, with payments settled directly into your bank account. Join Nepal’s largest digital payment network and take a step toward smarter, more efficient business operations.
