Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fonepay QR?

Fonepay QR is the most used QR service in Nepal. It is a secure and convenient digital payment solution that employs Quick Response (QR) codes, enabling users to scan QR codes with their smartphones.

How do I register as a Fonepay merchant?

You can enroll as a Fonepay merchant by visiting your Fonepay-supported banks.

Are there any fees for accepting QR payments in Nepal?

No, there are no fees for accepting QR payments in Nepal.

What’s the difference between static and dynamic QR codes?

Static QR is constant according to the merchant, while dynamic QR is unique for every bill.

Which banks and wallets support Fonepay QR payments?

Almost all banks and wallets support Fonepay QR payments in Nepal. You can check the supporting banks and wallets from Fonepay Partners.

Can Indian customers use UPI for QR payments in Nepal?

Yes, now Fonepay QR accepts UPI payments, making it convenient for Indian tourists.

How quickly do I receive settlements after a customer pays?

The service is very quick; you will receive notification within a second after a payment. Well, it also depends on the quality of your internet connection.

How many merchants in Nepal already use Fonepay QR?

Over 1.7 million merchants in Nepal are already using Fonepay QR.
