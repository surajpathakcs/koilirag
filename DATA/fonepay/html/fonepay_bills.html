Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fonepay Bills?

Fonepay Bills is one of the services of Fonepay through which customers can directly pay their bills for Electricity, Water, TV, Internet, EMI, Government Revenue, Education, Airlines, and more with just a few taps.

How can I purchase airline tickets using Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Domestic Flights” or “International Flight”.
  4. Choose “Airlines” & “Flight time”.
  5. Fill the necessary details.
  6. Check the details and proceed for “Payment”.

How can I pay electricity bills through Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Electricity” service.
  4. Fill your information of “NEA Counter”, “SC Number/ID” & proceed to “Inquiry”.
  5. Check the details and proceed for “Payment”.

How can I pay Internet bills through Fonepay Bills?

  1. Login to your mobile banking app.
  2. Navigate ‘Payments’ section and locate ‘Fonepay Bills’.
  3. Tap “Internet” service.
  4. Select your ISP service.
  5. Enter your username and select your package.
  6. Check the details and confirm “Payment”.
